Petition: Establish the AI Music Academy, Awards & Charts Board

We, the undersigned artists, songwriters, producers, engineers, developers, music professionals, and fans, call for the creation of an independent organization dedicated to AI-assisted music.

AI-assisted music should be recognized for its artistic excellence while held to meaningful standards of consent, disclosure, attribution, and fair compensation.

We support the formation of a nonprofit, globally representative AI Music Academy that will:

  • Present annual AI Music Awards that honor human-directed artistic and technical excellence.
  • Publish public eligibility rules and require transparent disclosure of relevant AI use.
  • Protect artists from unauthorized voice, likeness, and identity imitation.
  • Give independent artists, producers, engineers, and creators a real voice in governance.
  • Build education, resources, mentorship, and advocacy for the AI music community.

We also support an independent AI Music Charts Board that will publish verified rankings using transparent methodology, credible data partnerships, anti-fraud safeguards, and clear distinctions between popularity, artistic merit, and ethical disclosure.

Our belief: innovation and integrity can coexist. The next era of music should be creator-led, consent-based, transparent, and open to the world.

AI Music Awards

The awards should celebrate the people behind remarkable records, not simply the tools used. A song can be AI-assisted and still demand exceptional writing, direction, taste, editing, performance, mixing, mastering, and visual storytelling.

  • Song of the Year

  • Best Mixing & Mastering

  • Ethical AI Innovation Award

AI Music Charts Board

Charts should show what audiences are actually choosing. They must rely on verified data, explain their methodology, and actively resist bots, click farms, fake streams, and pay-to-play manipulation.

AI Music Hot 100

Weekly ranking of songs based on verified streaming, sales, video consumption, and eligible audience engagement.

AI Music Global 200

Worldwide ranking designed to reflect real cross-border listening and discovery.

AI Independent 100

A spotlight for self-released and independent artists building careers outside traditional gatekeepers.

AI Viral 50

A momentum chart for songs accelerating through short-form video, shares, creator adoption, and fan communities.

Non-negotiable standards

A credible AI music institution must make innovation accountable to creators and audiences.

✓
Consent first

No unauthorized use of a person’s recognizable voice, likeness, identity, or protected material.

◌
Transparent disclosure

Creators should accurately disclose relevant AI use and list human contributors and roles.

⚖
Independent governance

No single platform, label, technology company, or investor should control the institution.

⌁
Auditable charts

Methodologies, data sources, fraud safeguards, corrections, and appeals should be clear and accountable.
